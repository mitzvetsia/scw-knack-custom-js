How this model works

Team revenue by month

Each rep's ramp × attainment, summed, against booked revenue. Dashed lines: the half's target and break-even per month. Targets & thresholds are set on the Projections tab.

Reps

Attainment is per person per scenario — e.g. Zach 80% modest / 100% stretch. Base salaries live on Staffing; commissions pay on attained revenue. Leave Comm % blank and the rep pays from the tier table below at the rate their attainment earns (so modest and stretch can pay different rates); type a % for a flat override. Sales Manager and Director overrides are in the levers above. Someone leaving sales — or moving into a different role — gets a Last mo: their revenue and commissions stop after that month (for a role change, add the new role on the Staffing tab so their pay continues there).

Monthly revenue by rep

Each rep's modeled month-over-month revenue — ramp value for their tenure month × attainment. Use it to check people are plugged into the right curve and start month.

Ramp schedules (monthly run-rate by tenure month)

Payroll: model vs booked

Bars are Xero booked payroll (wages + tax + benefits, commissions included). The line is this tab's fully-loaded model — expect it to sit under the bars in strong commission months.

Raise planner

Every planned raise in one list — pick who it covers (a whole department, one role, or a named person), how much (% or dollars), and when it starts. Raises stack: a person covered by a department raise and their own raise gets both.

Roster & headcount plan

Fully-loaded monthly cost = (salary/12 + planned raises) × (1 + tax&match) + health + per-seat licenses. The two rates below are entered once and apply to every eligible role — untick BEN or TAX on a row for 1099 contractors or coverage decliners. Set headcount by month (later months follow), or open a role (▸) to name its people or switch it to hourly.

Pay bands

Annual earnings (hourly annualized) as the org stands in the chosen month: eliminated roles (headcount zero) and ended people drop off, and raises count only once they've landed. Click any dot to exclude that role or person outright; restore from the Excluded list below the chart. Jan ’26 = before everything; Mar ’27 = the full plan. One dot per role — the number inside is its headcount; a role with named people sits at their median, with each person's figure in the tooltip. Split-out departments keep their roles in their own bucket row.
