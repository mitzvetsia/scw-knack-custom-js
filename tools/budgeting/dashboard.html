How this model works
Plan

Locked plans

A locked plan is the whole P&L — every month, every department, every vendor — frozen as it stood. Lock one when the plan is agreed; later imports compare against it, and the live model keeps moving underneath.

Actuals

Import a Xero export

Drop an Account Transactions export (accrual basis, account types Expense & Revenue, any date range) here. It is read in this browser — nothing is uploaded. Vendors are normalised the same way the model's own history was; months the export covers become actuals for the comparison, and every vendor's last-billed date updates.

Reps

Attainment is per person per scenario — e.g. Zach 80% modest / 100% stretch. Base salaries live on Staffing; commissions pay on attained revenue. Leave Comm % blank and the rep pays from the tier table below at the rate their attainment earns (so modest and stretch can pay different rates); type a % for a flat override. Sales Manager and Director overrides are in the levers above.

Who is on this list comes from the staffing plan. Name someone on a Sales role there — a real name or a TBD — and they appear here, ramping from the month they join and stopping when they leave. Move someone between roles (end them on one, start them on the next) and each stint gets its own row on the right curve; an AM→AE move picks up the transition ramp automatically. What you set here stays yours: ramp, attainment, commission, and Ramped mo — months of ramp already behind them, which is how someone already at full rate in Jan ’26 avoids being modelled as a new hire.

Monthly revenue by rep

Each rep's modeled month-over-month revenue — the ramp value for their tenure month × their attainment. These are not quota figures: a fully-ramped AE carries $83K/mo, and at 80% modest attainment this grid shows $67K. The quota itself is the Full rate column on the ramp curves below. Shaded columns are already closed: the P&L takes their revenue straight from Xero, so the ramp figures there are a modelling check only.

Payroll: model vs booked

Bars are Xero booked payroll (wages + tax + benefits, commissions included). The line is this tab's fully-loaded model — expect it to sit under the bars in strong commission months.

Raise planner

Every planned raise in one list — pick who it covers (a whole department, one role, or a named person), how much (% or dollars), and when it starts. Raises stack: a person covered by a department raise and their own raise gets both.

Roster & headcount plan

Fully-loaded monthly cost = (salary/12 + planned raises) × (1 + tax&match) + health + per-seat licenses. The two rates below are entered once and apply to every eligible role — untick BEN or TAX on a row for 1099 contractors or coverage decliners. Set headcount by month (later months follow), or open a role (▸) to name its people or switch it to hourly. Each department header carries its own headcount by month, and the HEADCOUNT row at the foot totals the whole company — shaded months are booked, where payroll comes from Xero rather than this plan.

Pay bands

Annual earnings (hourly annualized) as the org stands in the chosen month: eliminated roles (headcount zero) and ended people drop off, and raises count only once they've landed. Click any dot to exclude that role or person outright; restore from the Excluded list below the chart. Jan ’26 = before everything; Mar ’27 = the full plan. One dot per role — the number inside is its headcount; a role with named people sits at their median, with each person's figure in the tooltip. Split-out departments keep their roles in their own bucket row.

Rules

Licenses & seats

One bill per vendor. Open a bill (▸) for its seat rules and department allocations, and add either from the bill line. Seats assigned to roles ride each head on Staffing; a rule scoped to every role follows the staffing plan as it changes; seats bought below a minimum land on the overflow department. Xero's trailing average sits beside each bill — every rule and allocation on it, compared once. How it works ↗

Planned $/mo = override if set, else the chosen source (Xero suggestion or budget-tool figure) × (1 + adj), months FROM–TO, when the switch is on. ONE-OFF rows plan nothing here: they draw on the department's account budget for their account (an “Account budget” row, added with + Budget an account), whose Xero suggestion is what vendors without a planned row booked to that account over the trailing year. Per-seat license costs ride headcount on the Staffing tab, not here. Where the budgeting tool's department disagreed with Xero, the budgeting tool's wins.
