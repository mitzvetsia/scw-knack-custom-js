How this model works

Team revenue by month

Each rep's ramp × attainment, summed, against booked revenue. Dashed lines: the half's target and break-even per month. Targets & thresholds are set on the Projections tab.

Reps

Attainment is per person per scenario — e.g. Zach 80% modest / 100% stretch. Commission % pays on attained revenue; Sales Manager and Director overrides are in the levers above.

Monthly revenue by rep

Each rep's modeled month-over-month revenue — ramp value for their tenure month × attainment. Use it to check people are plugged into the right curve and start month.

Ramp schedules (monthly run-rate by tenure month)

Payroll: model vs booked

Bars are Xero booked payroll (wages + tax + benefits, commissions included). The line is this tab's fully-loaded model — expect it to sit under the bars in strong commission months.

Raise planner

Every planned raise in one list — pick who it covers (a whole department, one role, or a named person), how much (% or dollars), and when it starts. Raises stack: a person covered by a department raise and their own raise gets both.

Roster & headcount plan

Fully-loaded monthly cost = (salary/12 + planned raises) × (1 + tax&match %) + health + per-seat licenses. Set headcount by month (later months follow), or open a role (▸) to name its people or switch it to hourly pay (rate × hrs/wk — shown normalized to $/yr and $/mo everywhere). Tax defaults to Xero's actual employer tax+match rate — resets every role to it.

Planned $/mo = override if set, else the chosen source (Xero suggestion or budget-tool figure) × (1 + adj), months FROM–TO, when the switch is on. Per-seat license costs (Slack, Google, Bonusly, seat portions of Hubspot/Gong/Zoom/Atlassian/Rippling/ClickUp) ride headcount on the Staffing tab, not here.
