Reads the CBP Form 7501: per entry line it sums the ad-valorem Chapter-99 + base HTS rates (MPF/HMF excluded), takes the base row's entered value, and validates rate × value against the printed duty. Invoice-level discounts are assumed allocated pro-rata by the broker (that's what the 7501 "entered value" already reflects).
