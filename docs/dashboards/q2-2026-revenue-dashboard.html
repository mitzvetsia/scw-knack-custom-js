How the goal maps. The $6.65M / $7.85M targets are for the Apr–Sep window — the “Q2 / Q3” column in the source sheet. The stretch figure is an exact match to the sheet’s own stretch scenario ($1.4M in Aug and Sep), which confirms the mapping.

What’s actual, what’s forecast. Jan through Jul 2026 are booked. Aug and Sep are the two scenarios carried in the sheet: $1.2M/mo at plan, $1.4M/mo at stretch.

Colour means pacing, everywhere on this page. Red is well behind the modest goal (under 85% of it), amber is behind modest, green clears modest, purple is stretch territory — within 10% of the stretch goal or past it. Every coloured figure also carries an icon and a word, so the colour is never doing the work on its own.

Part 2 formulas (reproduced exactly from the Daily KPIs sheet). Revenue to date = Install + Magento. Adjusted daily average = (revenue to date − big deal) ÷ business days elapsed. Projected = adjusted daily average × business days in month, with the big deal added back — so a single outsized deal lands in the total without inflating the run rate. “With pending” adds pending M2 and pending install to revenue to date before run-rating, matching the sheet’s $1,196,356. Required per day = (modest goal − revenue to date) ÷ business days left.

Business days elapsed accepts halves. The sheet’s 12 counts through end of yesterday; enter 12.5 if you are posting mid-day, or 13 for a completed day. Everything downstream — daily average, projection, activity pace — moves with it.

Two different revenue measures. Part 1 is booked revenue from the YoY Sales Metrics sheet ($11.6M in 2025). Parts 2 and 3 are total company revenue — Install services from Xero plus Magento — from the Daily KPIs sheet ($13.5M in 2025). They are not the same number and should not be added together. For 2022 and 2023 the two reconcile almost exactly once October is added back; for 2024 and 2025 a gap remains that is worth confirming before either figure is quoted as the company total.

Not carried over. The Daily KPIs sheet also holds a Jul / Aug / Sep quarter block whose August goal ($1,030,000) contradicts the current-month goal ($1,170,000), and a cautious / optimistic pair on a third basis. Both look stale, so neither is shown here.

Source & caveats. YoY Sales Metrics sheet. Monthly figures are reported rounded, so quarter totals can differ by a few tenths of a percent from the sheet’s own stated changes. Full-year totals are as reported and exclude October, which is absent from the source.
